Privacy Policy

Bolo

The short version. Bolo is designed for toddlers aged 2–5 and their parents. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All progress data stays on your child's device. The microphone is used only to detect whether your child made a sound — no recordings are ever saved or sent anywhere.

Who we are

Bolo is an independent, non-commercial project. It has no accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

What data Bolo collects

None. Bolo does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any contact info;
  • your child's name, age, photos, videos, or voice recordings;
  • your device identifier (IDFA/GAID) or advertising ID;
  • your location;
  • any usage analytics or behavioral data.

What Bolo stores on the device

To make the app work, Bolo stores a small amount of data locally on your child's device (never transmitted):

  • the age band you picked during setup ("2–3", "3–4", "4–5");
  • the count of words practiced today and all-time;
  • a session streak counter;
  • which categories your child has practiced in.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also reset it from the Parent Zone screen at any time.

Microphone permission

Bolo asks for microphone permission during setup so it can detect whether your child made a sound during a game. This detection runs entirely on the device using your operating system's built-in speech services. Audio is not recorded, not stored, and not sent anywhere. Bolo does not perform speech recognition, transcription, or pronunciation scoring in this version.

You can decline microphone access, or revoke it later in your device settings, without losing any app functionality — Bolo will still work, it just won't cheer specifically when your child speaks.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Bolo is designed for children under 13 and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because Bolo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about children (or anyone else), no parental consent flow is required. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated and a clear consent step added to the app.

Third parties

Bolo does not use third-party advertising networks, analytics, marketing, or tracking services. The app makes no network calls at runtime except, optionally, to a crash-reporting service (Sentry) that strips device identifiers before any error is sent. That crash reporting is currently disabled and will only be enabled after a separate compliance review.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the app version notes will call it out. The last-updated date below is authoritative.
