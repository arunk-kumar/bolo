## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Bolo is a calm, ad-free word-play space made for little ones aged 2 to 5.

Every round is short. Every tap is rewarded. Every word is chosen from
child-development research — the MacArthur-Bates CDI first-words list
and ASHA speech milestones — so what your toddler practices actually
matches how they grow.

What's inside
━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• 300 words across 8 friendly categories: animals, food, family, my
  body, colours, things, vehicles, and outside.
• Words automatically match your child's age band — 2-3, 3-4, or 4-5.
• Every word is spoken clearly and warmly, ready to imitate.
• Big, chunky buttons for little fingers. High-contrast art. No
  timers, no losing, no scary sounds.
• A parent zone (behind a small math gate) shows practice progress
  by category and a gentle streak counter.

What's NOT inside
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• No accounts. No login. No email address needed.
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No paywalls.
• No trackers. No analytics. No third-party services.
• No cloud sync. All progress stays on the device.

Privacy first
━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Bolo asks for microphone permission during setup so it can cheer when
your child speaks. That detection runs entirely on the device — no
audio is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. You can skip the mic
permission and the app still works.

Full privacy policy:
https://arunk-kumar.github.io/bolo/privacy/

Made for the moments before the words come
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every toddler grows at their own pace. Bolo isn't a therapy tool and
isn't a clinical assessment — it's a small, quiet space where a child
can practice saying words with their parent nearby, and be cheered on
every time they try.

Hindi and other Indian-language packs are coming in future updates.
```

*(~1930 chars — comfortably under 4000)*

## Store graphics required

| Asset            | Dimensions   | Format | Notes                                    |
|------------------|--------------|--------|------------------------------------------|
| App icon         | 512×512      | PNG    | Uses `app/assets/branding/icon.png` (upscale) |
| Feature graphic  | 1024×500     | PNG    | Wide banner — see feature_graphic.png     |
| Phone screenshot | 1080×1920 min| PNG    | 2 – 8 screenshots                        |

Screenshots to capture:
1. **Stage map** — the vertical journey through categories.
2. **Naming game** — a big word card mid-round.
3. **Session complete** — the celebration screen.
4. **Progress dashboard** — parent-zone stat tiles.
5. *(optional)* **Onboarding age picker** — first-launch calm.

Use Chrome DevTools device mode at `iPhone 14 Pro Max` (430×932) or
`Pixel 7 Pro` (412×892) — either satisfies Play's minimum. Screenshot
via macOS `Cmd+Shift+4` targeting the browser viewport.
